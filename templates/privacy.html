Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records, categories, and amounts you log in Spendly.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the core features of Spendly — storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted infrastructure providers solely to host and operate the service.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
